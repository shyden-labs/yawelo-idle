## Choices

- **"Bantuan", not Google's "Mendukung".** Google's en→id rendering of the one-word title is the verb "to support", which does not work as a page title. "Bantuan" (help) is the usual title of an Indonesian help or support page, and the read-back gives "Help". The form and ticket lines use "bantuan" too, so the page's words agree with each other.
- **"tentang" (about) for "with".** "masalah dengan Yawelo Idle" (Google) is literal; "masalah tentang" reads as a problem concerning the game. The read-back keeps the meaning ("an issue about Yawelo Idle").
- **"tidak dapat dimuat" (could not be loaded)** where Google wrote "tidak dimuat" (was not loaded): the read-back keeps the meaning, and it states the failure, not only the state.
- **Label audit (3 words or fewer):** "Bantuan" and "Tiket bantuan Anda" read back as "Help" and "Your support ticket", with no other sense in context.
