## Reading of the read-back

- `intro`: same meaning. "Here's the progress of" for "This is where the work … stands"; "without needing to be refreshed" for "with no need to reload".
- `launch`: same meaning, near word for word.
- `howToRead`: same meaning, sentence by sentence. "may go up or down" for "can fall as well as rise"; "an estimate based on the progress of the last seven days" for "a forecast from the last seven days' pace". "Larger projects" reads back for "pekerjaan yang lebih besar" (a bigger job), the same idea.
- `englishNote`: same meaning.

## Second rendering (Google en→id, 2026-11-15)

Google rendered the four approved English lines into Indonesian independently (612 characters, `scripts/google-read-back.ts en id`). Each says the same as the drafted line; the differences are word choice only:

- `intro`: Google has "Informasi ini diperbarui secara otomatis seiring berjalannya pengerjaan" where the draft has "Halaman ini diperbarui sendiri saat pekerjaan berjalan": both "updates by itself as the work moves".
- `launch`: Google keeps the loanword "browser"; the draft has "peramban", the standard Indonesian word. "hari di mana" (Google) and "hari ketika" (draft) both mean "the day when"; "ketika" is the more formal choice.
- `howToRead`: Google has "permainan" and "kecepatan"; the draft has "game" and "laju" (pace). Same meaning.
- `englishNote`: identical.
