## Reading of the read-back

Every read-back says the same as the approved English. Word-level differences:

- `mapLabel`: "Voyage to launch". Google's own rendering "Perjalanan untuk meluncurkan" reads as "a trip in order to launch (something)"; "Pelayaran" keeps the voyage-by-sea picture the map draws.
- `state.underWay`: "In progress". Google renders "Sedang berlangsung", a synonym of "Sedang berjalan".
- `panel.brings`: "What this stage presents". Google's "Apa yang ditimbulkan oleh hal ini" means "what this causes", wrong for a list of features.
- `panel.close`: read back "Closed". "Tutup" is the standard Indonesian word on a close button (the imperative); Google's "Menutup" is the verb form "closing", not a button label.
- `bar`: "of work towards launch is complete". Same meaning.
- `seasonNote`: same meaning, sentence by sentence; Google's rendering adds "hanyalah" (merely) and "seiring berjalannya pengembangan" (as development goes on).
- `features.m3.cosy`: "A comfortable idle game to revisit again and again". "Nyaman" is the usual word for cosy; Google's rendering adds "santai" (relaxed).
- `features.m3.yourWay`: "Play your way". Google uses the informal "-mu"; the site uses the polite "Anda" throughout.
- `features.m4`: "Your progress will never be lost". Google's rendering, "Jangan pernah kehilangan kemajuan Anda", is an order ("never lose your progress!"); the shipped line is the promise the English means.
- `features.m6.grammar`: "final round"; Google's rendering "babak final" means the same.
- `features.m6.free`: "peramban" is the standard Indonesian word for browser, as the site already ships (#830's `launch` line).
- `features.m8`: "Available in app stores". Google's "Di toko-toko" (in shops) loses that these are app stores.

## Label audit (lines of 3 words or fewer)

- `state.done` "Selesai", `state.notStarted` "Belum dimulai", `features.m3.journeys` "Perjalanan dan budaya", `features.m3.music` "Musik dan suara", `features.m5` "Papan peringkat dan teman": identical in both Google directions.
- `panel.close` "Tutup": see above; it is the label on close buttons across Indonesian interfaces.
- `features.m3.stick` "Kata-kata yang melekat di ingatan": read back "Words that stick in memory"; Google's "Kata-kata yang membekas" (words that leave a mark) is the same idea.
- `features.m7` "Aplikasi desktop dan ponsel": read back "Desktop and mobile applications"; "ponsel" is the common word for a mobile phone, Google's "telepon" is a telephone in general.
- `features.m8`: see above.

Island names (the epics' Public titles) and "Yawelo Idle" stay English on the Indonesian page; `islandNamesNote` says so.
